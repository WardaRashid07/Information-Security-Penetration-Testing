Q3: What is the principle of least privilege and how does it apply to managing running services on an attack VM?

A3: The principle of least privilege means only running necessary services and giving only required permissions. On an attack VM, unnecessary services like Bluetooth, printing (cups), and Avahi should be disabled to reduce the attack surface and minimize forensic traces during penetration testing.
